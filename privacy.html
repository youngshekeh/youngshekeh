Privacy Policy

THE FATHER ANALYTICS uses account, subscription, support and product-usage data only to operate, secure and improve the service. Authentication credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. Payment card details are handled by the configured payment provider and are not stored by this frontend.

Private member and owner surfaces are designed to expose only the minimum data required for the authenticated workflow. Server-side authorization remains authoritative for paid entitlements and owner access.

For privacy or account requests, use the authenticated Account Center or official support channel.
