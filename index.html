03 · ACCOUNTABLE INTELLIGENCE

No black-box certainty. Every conclusion leaves a trail.

The system is designed to explain what it knows, what it infers, what remains unavailable and how a forecast performed after reality arrived.

A

Evidence-gated ingestion

Sources, freshness and availability are separated from interpretation. Missing evidence stays missing.

B

Causal world model

Signals are connected through cause-and-effect pathways instead of headline accumulation.

C

Scenario & solution lab

Alternative paths expose trade-offs, unintended consequences, constraints and measurable objectives.

D

Forecast accountability

Forecasts freeze before outcomes, then accuracy, calibration and failure attribution are measured.

E

Immutable publication snapshots

Published states preserve what the system knew at the time instead of rewriting history after the fact.

F

Autonomous health monitoring

Runtime, data integrity, evidence stores and readiness gates are monitored separately and fail closed when verification disappears.
