Rules above impulse.

01 · Intelligence before decisions.No action outruns data quality, provenance, or the governing evidence.

03 · Uncertainty has a state.Missing or conflicting evidence becomes WAIT, not an invented answer.
04 · Real capital requires human release.No dashboard, watcher, model or alert can silently unlock live money.
