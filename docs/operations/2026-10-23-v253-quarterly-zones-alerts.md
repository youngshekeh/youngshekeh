# V253 · Quarterly Liquidity Proxies and Opt-In Alerts

**Scope:** nine tracked research instruments: Gold COMEX futures GC=F (NOT XAU spot), Bitcoin Coinbase Exchange BTC-USD spot, Brent BZ=F and WTI CL=F futures (historically continuous rolled symbols), EURUSD=X spot reference, ICE US Dollar Index DX-Y.NYB, S&P 500 ^GSPC, Nasdaq Composite ^IXIC (NOT Nasdaq 100), and the Cboe US 10Y yield indicator ^TNX (NOT cash 10-year Treasury bond). All nine are different measures; no price level is transferred across symbols.

**Data calculation:** completed previous UTC quarter daily OHLC high and low with 2%-of-quarter-range inner bands at each extreme, previous-quarter midpoint and current UTC quarter-to-date high/low. Every month in the completed quarter requires at least 16 independently observed daily candles for non-crypto instruments, 27 for BTC. OHLC data is validated for duplicates, missing prices, symbols, venue and sample coverage. These are *price-range-derived potential liquidity proxies only*, not observed stop orders, Level 2 book, institutional positions, market-maker flow, forecasts or execution instructions. Bars taken from Yahoo's rolling futures series may have contract-roll discontinuities. No calibrated probability reported.

**Source truth:** Read-only GET `/api/public-quarterly-zones` retrieves fixed 6-month daily OHLC from Yahoo Finance for eight instruments and bounded Coinbase Exchange daily BTC OHLC covering the completed quarter and QTD. Provider calls are parallel and independent with 5.5s upstream timeout and positive cache <=8 minutes; packets expire at 12 minutes. Quotes must independently be provider-timestamped within 65 min for Yahoo indicators, and Bitcoin receipt within 30 seconds through existing Coinbase retail spot API. Delayed Yahoo pricing is not broker-grade or guaranteed realtime. Public historical data redistribution/commercial licensing remains **UNVERIFIED**, research evaluation only.

**Alerts:** On the Market Observatory page, user explicitly clicks `Enable tab alerts` for alerts of entering upper/lower quarterly bands or moving above/below prior-quarter high/low. The rule engine enforces exact quote-source identity, freshness, 0R status, per-asset transitions and a 10-minute duplicate-event cooldown. User may separately choose `Allow browser notifications` from a direct click for desktop notifications while the dashboard tab is open. No persistent background push, SMS, email, server-side alarm queue, customer contacts or guaranteed timely alerting. Tab history is limited to 60 records in memory and cleared on navigation/reload.

**Existing controls:** 58 pg_cron schedules, all private Supabase Edge functions, trading permission WAIT/0R, live orders OFF, Gold API source validation, and subscription/commerce launch gates preserved. No database or secret changes. Commercial distribution remains BUILD HOLD.
