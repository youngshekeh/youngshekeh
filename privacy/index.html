PRIVACY

Privacy principles.

THE FATHER ANALYTICS is designed to separate public intelligence from authenticated member and owner data. Private account data should only be used to provide the requested service, maintain security, support billing and operate permitted product features.

Data minimization

We aim to collect only information necessary for account access, security, service delivery, billing and product improvement.

Authentication and security

Authentication is handled through the connected identity infrastructure. Owner access uses stronger authorization and MFA controls where enabled.

Payments

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. Sensitive payment credentials are not intended to be stored directly in the public frontend.

Contact and controls

Member-facing data export, deletion-request and account controls may be made available through the member workspace as the platform matures.
